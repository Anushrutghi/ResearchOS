# v3 — ResearchOS v0.4 Methodology and Scientific Study Design

**Status: Complete (v0.4 acceptance passed; advanced methods remain follow-up work)**

## Scope

Build problem analysis, research question and hypothesis drafting, editable study designs, feasibility and missing-information checks, variable/population/sampling definitions, limitations and ethics prompts, and domain-aware methodology packs for quantitative, qualitative, mixed, computational, theoretical, systematic-review, CS/ML/cybersecurity/physics/medical work. Statistical recommendations must explain assumptions and applicability; medical and human-subject work requires expert and ethics oversight.

## Acceptance checks

- Research questions trace to a documented problem and relevant literature.
- Recommendations explain their rationale and assumptions.
- Study designs are structured, editable, and require researcher approval.
- Unsupported assumptions and missing inputs are flagged; no research gap is invented to imply novelty.

## Implemented so far

- Typed research-problem analysis records, explicit supported research types, and conservative domain identification for computer science, ML, cybersecurity, physics, medical, and social-science fields.
- Deterministic, rationale-bearing methodology packs that are filtered by the explicit research type. Missing problem statements, objectives, questions, and research type are flagged instead of generated.
- Conditional statistical guidance for common continuous, binary, categorical, count, ordinal, and time-to-event outcomes across several designs. Independent-group methods are withheld unless independence is confirmed; candidates expose assumptions and missing checks and never calculate results.
- Optional, validated researcher-supplied dataset summaries produce descriptive prompts for sample-count mismatch, group-size imbalance, missingness, event counts, and count mean/variance/zeros. They do not compute inferential results, power, or adequacy.
- Structured local JSON study-design drafts with objectives, questions, hypotheses, population/material, sampling, variables, conditions, data collection, analysis, metrics, limitations, ethics notes, and links to reviewed literature claims.
- Draft edit/save and explicit researcher approval. Approval checks required fields, methodology rationale, limitations, ethics notes, and source-reviewed literature; approved records are immutable.
- Browser workflow for problem analysis, domain recommendations, statistical assumption checks, editable study designs, and approval review.
- Optional OpenAI-compatible research-question and hypothesis refinement from an explicit problem statement, objectives, research type, and selected reviewed claims. The provider receives only the selected claim text/source passage; returned items must cite selected claim IDs, are validated server-side, remain proposed/editable, and include source page links in the UI. The prompt treats passages as untrusted data, prohibits invented gaps/novelty/citations, and allows empty results with cautions.
- Research-type-specific feasibility and sampling checklists for quantitative, qualitative, mixed-methods, computational, theoretical, and systematic-review work. Additional checks cover medical oversight, participant privacy/access, ML split feasibility, authorized cybersecurity test environments, and physics instrument/calibration access. The guidance does not generate a numeric sample size or assert that a design is feasible.

## Feedback and limits

- The current recommendations are deterministic domain packs, not a substitute for a domain statistician or ethics review. AI-assisted refinement requires configured provider access and is not evidence of novelty or correctness; the researcher must review each proposed wording and its linked source.
- Statistical candidates remain limited. Bayesian methods, clustered/repeated models, and power/sample-size planning remain future work. Summary diagnostics rely on researcher-supplied aggregates and do not inspect raw observations.
- Literature grounding requires selecting a claim already reviewed in the v0.3 evidence workspace. There is no automatic research-gap detector and no cross-project traceability yet.
- Design revisions are editable while draft; approved designs are immutable. Revision history and project-level permissions are future work.
